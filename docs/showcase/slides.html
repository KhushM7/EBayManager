Safety

Rules enforced in code, not prompts

  • One write gateOnly ebayWrite() may call eBay; tests fail the build if anything else does.
  • Locked kindsPrices, messages, offers and new listings can never auto-apply.
  • Floors and stepsThe owner's floor in code; at most 10% a price step.
  • Hidden siteBlank 404 for strangers; email plus authenticator code.
  • Read-only Power PlatformA scoped key that can't write, approve or see buyers.
  • Sandbox firstLive only after go-live tests A to H passed.

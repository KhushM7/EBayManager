EBayManager privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026.

EBayManager is a private tool used by one eBay seller and at most two people who help manage their account. It is not a public service and has no other users. Nobody can sign in except those three people.

What it stores

  • The seller's own listings: titles, prices, item specifics, descriptions, watcher and view counts.
  • The seller's own orders: order totals, fees, item numbers and dates. Buyer names and addresses are not stored.
  • Questions buyers send about the seller's listings, and Best Offers: the buyer's eBay username and the text of their question or the offer amount, so a reply can be drafted.
  • Records of every change the seller approved, and who approved it.

Who processes it

  • Microsoft Azure hosts the app and its database, in the EU (France Central).
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads listing details and buyer questions to draft suggestions. Nothing is sent to buyers without the seller's approval.
  • Two sold-price data providers, Trawl and CompsAPI, receive only search words such as a product name. They receive no eBay user names, item numbers or account details.

No data is sold or shared for advertising.

Deletion

The app follows eBay's marketplace account deletion notifications. When an eBay user closes their account, everything the app holds about them, including their username and messages, is deleted or removed from its records.

Data about buyers is kept only as long as it is needed to answer them and to keep a record of the seller's own sales.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the seller through eBay messages.
